# SaMD Module — Clinical Journey Intelligence

## Intended purpose
To reconstruct an authorized longitudinal clinical journey and identify care/follow-up gaps or missing expected events for professional review.

## Not intended
Not a diagnosis engine and not an autonomous care-ordering system.

## Risk classification
TBD after formal intended-purpose and clinical risk assessment.

## Clinical safety & validation
Validate event linkage, chronology, patient matching, gap detection, missing-data behavior and alert precision. Test incomplete/duplicate/out-of-order records and cross-facility reconciliation. Prospective workflow validation should establish whether signals improve timely review without creating unsafe automation bias.

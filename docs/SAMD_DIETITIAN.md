# SaMD Module — Dietitian Intelligence

## Intended purpose
To analyze anthropometry, dietary intake, relevant conditions/laboratory data and medication context and produce nutrition-risk, trajectory and diet-planning decision-support signals for dietitian/clinical review.

## Not intended
No autonomous therapeutic diet prescription or replacement for dietitian assessment.

## Risk classification
TBD after formal intended-purpose and clinical risk assessment.

## Clinical safety & validation
Validate anthropometric calculations, nutrient calculations, missing-data behavior, disease-context rules, adherence measures and scenario outputs. Assess subgroup performance, usability, inappropriate dietary recommendations and medication/nutrition conflicts. Prospective review by dietitians is required before deployment.

# SaMD Module — Laboratory Intelligence

## Intended purpose
To analyze authorized laboratory observations and longitudinal laboratory trends and produce evidence-linked signals for professional review.

## Not intended
Not an autonomous diagnosis, treatment order, or replacement for the laboratory report.

## Risk classification
Prototype status: **TBD pending formal intended-purpose, jurisdiction and risk assessment**. Do not assign a legal class from this repository alone.

Risk analysis should cover false negative/positive signals, critical-value handling, unit/reference-range errors, missing data, delayed data, wrong-patient linkage and model drift.

## Clinical safety & validation
- analytical verification of parsing/unit/reference range
- retrospective clinical validation against adjudicated laboratory cases
- sensitivity/specificity/PPV/NPV where applicable
- critical-value alert sensitivity
- subgroup performance by age/sex/facility where relevant
- usability and alert-fatigue assessment
- prospective silent-mode evaluation before clinical action
- post-deployment monitoring and change control

# SaMD Module — Patient Clinical Summary

## Intended purpose
To generate a role-scoped, evidence-traceable summary from authorized longitudinal clinical records for professional review.

## Not intended
Not the source medical record and not an autonomous clinical decision maker.

## Risk classification
TBD. Classification depends on the actual intended purpose, whether outputs influence clinical decisions, and applicable jurisdictional rules.

## Clinical safety & validation
Validate factual completeness, omission rate, source traceability, temporal correctness, role-based data minimization, hallucination rate, contradiction detection and clinician usability. Require source-linked evidence and human verification before clinical action.

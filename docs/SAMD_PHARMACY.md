# SaMD Module — Pharmacy Intelligence

## Intended purpose
To analyze medication records, dispensing/administration history and relevant laboratory/condition context to produce medication-safety, adherence and utilization signals for pharmacist/clinician review.

## Not intended
No autonomous prescribing, discontinuation, dose change or final interaction determination.

## Risk classification
TBD after formal intended-purpose and clinical risk assessment.

## Clinical safety & validation
Validate duplicate detection, interaction knowledge-base accuracy, medication linkage, refill-gap calculations and adverse-event signals. Test false alerts, missed safety signals, data latency, wrong-patient/wrong-medication linkage, terminology changes and knowledge-base versioning. Prospective silent-mode and pharmacist adjudication are required before actioning alerts.
